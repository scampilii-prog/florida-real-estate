SMARTER COASTAL BUYING

What to Verify Before You Make an Offer

Two homes with similar asking prices can have very different ownership costs and risks. Use these Florida Gulf Coast checks alongside the property disclosures, your inspection contingency and professional advice.

Flood & Insurance

Check the current FEMA flood designation, available elevation information, property-specific insurance quotes and hurricane or wind-mitigation features. Insurance availability and premiums vary by property.

HOA, Condo & CDD

Review restrictions, budgets, fees, assessments, reserves and relevant inspection reports. For condos, ask about milestone inspections, Structural Integrity Reserve Studies when applicable, and pending assessments.

Property & Closing

Discuss inspections, permits, roof and mechanical systems, survey or boundary matters, title exceptions, homestead eligibility and projected closing costs with the appropriate professionals.

Waterfront buyer? Verify seawall condition, dock permits, bridge clearance, water depth and boating access independently; a canal-front property is not automatically open-water accessible.

What Matters Most When Buying on Florida's Gulf Coast

Beyond the asking price, coastal homeownership involves important questions about insurance, property condition, association rules and financing. Explore these focused guides before making a decision.

Financing: Pre-Approval, DU & LPA →Understand what lenders verify, automated underwriting findings and why final approval is conditional.Inspections & Appraisals →Evaluate property condition, hurricane-related features, insurance considerations and appraisal issues.Communities, Condos & Waterfront →Consider location, flood exposure, HOA or condo restrictions, taxes and total ownership costs.From Contract to Closing →Review contract milestones, title work, financing conditions and closing coordination.

Florida condo milestone inspections, reserve requirements and association records may matter for some properties. Ask for current documents and qualified professional advice rather than assuming the same requirements apply to every building.

Florida Gulf Coast Due Diligence

Check flood zones and elevation, flood and wind insurance quotes, roof age, storm history, permits, condition and wind-mitigation information. For waterfront homes, investigate seawalls, docks, water depth, bridge clearance and navigation.

For condos, review budgets, reserves, assessments, governing documents, insurance, and any applicable milestone inspections and structural integrity reserve studies. Requirements vary by property.

Arrange independent inspections and verify documents within contractual review periods.
