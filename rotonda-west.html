Living in Rotonda West, Florida

Rotonda West is a planned residential community in Charlotte County, Southwest Florida. Recognized for its distinctive circular layout, golf courses, freshwater canals and quiet neighborhoods, it offers an appealing lifestyle for year-round residents and seasonal homeowners. Nearby destinations include Englewood, Cape Haze, Placida and Boca Grande.
