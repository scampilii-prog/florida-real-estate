Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Learn how contact inquiries, email messages, language preferences, hosting services and third-party resources may process information on this Florida Gulf Coast real estate website.

Who operates this website

This site is operated for real estate information and inquiries by Sabatino Campilii, Realtor® (Florida License #SL3363040), affiliated with LoKation® Real Estate. Privacy questions: sabatino@floridacostiera.com.

Information you choose to provide

If you email us, you may provide your name, email address, telephone number, preferred communities, budget, timeline, and details of your real estate request. Please do not send Social Security numbers, bank statements, identification documents, payment-card details or other highly sensitive information through ordinary email.

How the current forms work

The contact and property-preferences forms currently use your device's email application to prepare a message for you to review and send. Completing a form alone does not transmit its answers to Sabatino or store them in a website database. Once you send an email, your email provider and the recipient's email service process and retain that communication under their own practices.

How information is used

Information voluntarily sent is used to respond to inquiries, discuss requested real estate services, arrange follow-up and maintain relevant business communications. Information may be shared with the brokerage or transaction service providers when reasonably necessary and lawful; this page does not authorize selling personal information.

Hosting, technical information and external services

The website is hosted through Cloudflare Pages. Hosting providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser data, security events and access logs to deliver and protect the site. The site may load third-party resources such as Google Fonts, Google Translate and Unsplash photographs. Those providers may receive technical connection information under their own policies. Property search links and other external services have separate privacy policies.

Cookies and language preferences

Language settings may be remembered in your browser's local storage. Google Translate or other third-party tools may use cookies or similar technologies. This policy does not claim that no cookies or analytics are used; integrations may change. Browser controls may let you manage cookies or local storage.

The privacy notice and your choices

The first-visit privacy notice explains browser storage and third-party services. Selecting “Got it” only dismisses that notice and remembers your acknowledgment on this browser; it does not grant permission for advertising, analytics, or the sale or sharing of personal information. Use “Privacy notice” in the footer to reopen it. Browser settings let you manage cookies and site storage; clearing storage may remove saved language preferences or timeline work.

Appointment scheduling is provided by SimplyBook.me, and Ask Tino sends submitted questions to a third-party service through a website relay. Those services may process your submitted information and technical connection data. Use them only with information you are comfortable providing, and avoid sensitive personal or financial documents. For questions about these services or privacy requests, email sabatino@floridacostiera.com.

Retention and security

Messages you send may remain in business email records as reasonably needed for inquiries, transaction documentation and legal obligations. Reasonable precautions should be used, but no online transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. The website does not currently offer a dedicated secure upload portal for sensitive documents.

Your choices and requests

You may request information about communications you have sent, ask for corrections, or request deletion where applicable law permits by emailing sabatino@floridacostiera.com. Some records may need to be retained for legal or brokerage compliance. You can stop marketing communications by following any applicable unsubscribe instructions or contacting us.

Children and changes

The website is intended for adults considering real estate services, not for children under 13. This policy may be updated when forms, analytics, advertising or other services change. Material changes should be reflected in this notice before new collection practices begin.

Contact

For privacy questions, access or correction requests, contact Sabatino Campilii at sabatino@floridacostiera.com. Brokerage affiliation: LoKation® Real Estate.
