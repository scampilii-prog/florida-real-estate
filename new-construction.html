Before You Sign a Builder Contract

Compare the total price, lot premiums, options, upgrade costs, deposits, financing incentives, closing fees, HOA or CDD assessments and insurance. Builder incentives may depend on a preferred lender or settlement provider.

Review cancellation provisions, estimated completion, possible delays, warranties, independent inspection rights, and financing or appraisal conditions. Check what features are included versus shown as upgrades.

Get written answers before committing, and review legal, financing and inspection issues with qualified professionals.
