BEFORE YOUR HOME SEARCH

Get Prepared to Buy

A little preparation helps you make confident decisions when the right property appears. Whether you plan to finance your purchase or pay cash, these four steps are a practical starting point.

01 · YOUR BUDGET

Know Your Buying Power

For financing, discuss pre-approval with your lender and whether automated underwriting findings (DU or LPA) are appropriate. Cash buyers can prepare recent, appropriately redacted proof of funds. Neither preliminary findings nor pre-approval guarantee final loan approval.

02 · REPRESENTATION

Choose Your Buyer's Agent

Discuss representation and written buyer-broker agreement terms before touring properties. Your agent can explain the process, evaluate comparable sales and negotiate on your behalf. Compensation and representation terms are agreed in writing.

03 · PROPERTY SEARCH

Compare Before You Commit

Prioritize location, lifestyle and property condition. Review flood exposure, insurance quotes, HOA or condo restrictions, taxes and inspection considerations alongside the asking price.

04 · STRONG OFFERS

Prepare a Well-Supported Offer

Use comparable sales, suitable financing documentation, realistic timelines and clear contract terms. Your lender handles financial qualification and sensitive documents through appropriate channels.

HOW THE PIECES FIT TOGETHER

Working Together for a Successful Purchase

Sabatino's Role as Your Florida Buyer's Agent

  • Help you identify properties and communities that fit your goals
  • Explain local market conditions, comparable sales and ownership considerations
  • Help you evaluate offers, negotiate terms and understand contract deadlines
  • Guide you through inspections, title matters and the steps toward closing
  • Coordinate with your lender, title company and other professionals as appropriate

Your Mortgage Lender's Role

  • Review your finances and explain available loan programs and costs
  • Determine loan eligibility and assess debt-to-income and credit requirements
  • Issue pre-approval when appropriate and arrange underwriting and appraisal
  • Verify funds and income securely and provide financing documentation
  • Make the final lending decision, subject to underwriting and loan conditions

When your real estate agent and mortgage lender communicate effectively, you can make more informed decisions and prepare for a smoother purchase. A pre-approval is not a guarantee of final loan approval.

Buying with Cash?

You do not need mortgage approval, but it is wise to prepare suitable proof of available funds, understand closing costs and reserves, and evaluate inspections, insurance, title and any HOA or condo requirements. Discuss your purchase plans with Sabatino →
