YOUR REAL ESTATE ADVISOR

Sabatino Campilii

Realtor® · LoKation® Real Estate
Florida License #SL3363040 · Licensed since 2000

With more than 25 years across real estate, engineering, construction and development, Sabatino combines practical property knowledge with personal attention throughout the transaction. He helps buyers look beyond finishes and photographs, and helps sellers evaluate pricing, preparation, marketing and negotiations.

His experience extends from South Florida to the Gulf Coast, with a focus on Boca Grande, Gasparilla Island, Cape Haze, Englewood, Rotonda West, Venice, Wellen Park, Nokomis, Casey Key, Punta Gorda Isles and surrounding communities.

Whether the goal is a waterfront residence, a new-construction home, a condominium or an investment property, the approach remains the same: clear information, careful evaluation and straightforward communication.
