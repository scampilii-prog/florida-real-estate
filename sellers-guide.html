Evaluate the Whole Offer

Compare earnest money, financing type, appraisal and inspection terms, concessions, association approval, closing date, possession and other lawful conditions. The highest price alone may not be the strongest offer.

Lender verification and limited proof of closing funds may help assess a financed offer. Handle personal information securely and apply qualification requests consistently without regard to Fair Housing protected characteristics.

Compare estimated proceeds and the likelihood that each offer can close under its proposed terms.
